# Angry Birds Space

## What is randomized?

- **Episodes**: every episode except Pig Bang starts locked. Finding an episode item (for example
  *Cold Cuts Episode*) opens it on the episode map.
- **Birds**: a level can only be played once you have every bird it uses. You start with the Red Bird
  and the Blue Birds; the Lazer Bird, Firebomb Bird, Ice Bird, Big Brother Bird and Globe Bird are items.
- **Filler**: Space Eagles and the Space Egg, Flock of Birds and Pig Puffer power-ups.
- **Traps** (optional): the *Bird Swap Trap* swaps the bird on your slingshot for another kind from the level.

Locked episodes and levels show the game's own padlocks. Clicking a locked level tells you what it still
needs (for example "Needs: Ice Bird, Lazer Bird"). Items you send or receive pop up as in-game notifications.

## Locations

- Clearing each level in Pig Bang, Cold Cuts, Fry Me to the Moon, Utopia, Red Planet, Pig Dipper,
  Cosmic Crystals, Beak Impact 1 & 2, Brass Hogs, Danger Zone and (optionally) Eggsteroids: 309 levels.
- *Episode* Complete: clearing every level of an episode.
- With *Three Star Checks* on, three-starring a level is a second check.

## Goal

- **Boss Rush**: defeat all 8 episode bosses.
- **Level Count**: clear the number of levels set in *Levels For Goal*.
- **Star Hunt**: collect the number of stars set in *Stars For Goal* (your best stars per level count).

## DeathLink

Failing a level sends a death. Receiving one kicks you back to the level selection screen.
